The Veiled Isle — vertical slice concept

Unlockable fog island beyond progressed seafaring. Nordic, child-appropriate, practical Wayfinder Rune reward. Proposal only — not in production.

Fog uses local falloff around beacons/player. No fullscreen expensive filter.

Exploration layers

  1. Shore cove — readable landing, wreck timber, safe birds.
  2. Lichen heath — mist pockets, path findings via waystones.
  3. Beacon terrace — three fire bowls; lighting thins fog inland.
  4. Sealed hollow — multi-phase boss arena revealed after bowls lit.

Enemies (fair telegraphs)

Mist-moth swarm

Pulse glow, then dart. Answer with torch gaps / net.

Driftwood wight

Arms raise 0.6s before shove. Sidestep + collect.

Fog hound

Paw-scrape dust line. Bait or Skölli distract.

False guide echo

Friendly outline, delayed voice. Ignore its marked paths.

Boss phases — Mirrorkeel of the Sealed Beacon

1 · Veil

Slow arcs while lighting bowls.

2 · Mirror

False Haigen/companion; hit the one without warm shadow.

3 · Seal

Core open windows; timed hits. Drops Wayfinder Rune.
